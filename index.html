Overview

Exec summary

The quarter centred on three priorities: launching the Active Cash Fund ETF (FSCF) on the ASX, amplifying the Buy Hold Sell World Cup edition with Livewire, and rolling out refreshed ASX:LEVR messaging for the Geared Australian Share Fund.

The Cash ETF launch on 7 May, supported by a campaign across AFR, Investor Daily and Morningstar, lifted website visitors to around 50,000, up 25% on Q1. Eight launch emails reached 740 organisations, the broadest single-campaign footprint of the quarter.

In search we now appear for 145 Australian terms, up 40% year on year, though Vanguard still leads every category, so the priority for Q3 is embedding the brand into AI and LLM search results using the new website.

Global Listed Infrastructure content continued to resonate with Asian institutions, drawing more than 1,500 clicks from Asian banks, with DBS and HSBC among the firms reading our pages. Client engagement softened, with 1,730 contacts across 1,095 organisations, down 10% on Q1 as paid acquisition wound down.
