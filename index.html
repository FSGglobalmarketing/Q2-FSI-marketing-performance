Overview

Exec summary

The ETF launch drove reach, but search is flat and conversion softened.

FSI drew roughly 50,000 website visitors, up a quarter on Q1, led by the Cash Fund ETF listing on the ASX in May, and the Buy Hold Sell LinkedIn post pulled more than 6,000 clicks.

Search held flat on the quarter, up 40% year on year, as the market compressed: Australian Equities Growth moved ahead of Betashares, but Vanguard still leads every category and the big ETF issuers out-spend us on paid.

The ETF push landed with Asian institutions, with 1,500-plus GLIS clicks from Asian banks and Alphix confirming DBS, HSBC and UOB among the firms reading our pages.

Conversion softened: we engaged 1,730 contacts across 1,095 organisations, down 10% on Q1 as paid acquisition wound down.
